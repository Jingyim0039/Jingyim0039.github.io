I am a Ph.D. researcher in Transportation Engineering at **South China University of Technology (SCUT)** and a visiting Ph.D. researcher at **KTH Royal Institute of Technology** in Stockholm, Sweden.

My research lies at the intersection of **intelligent transportation systems, traffic computer vision, multimodal artificial intelligence, and traffic prediction**.

My current work focuses on developing intelligent methods for traffic perception, congestion detection and prediction, multimodal traffic understanding, and large language models for transportation applications.

### Research Interests

- Intelligent Transportation Systems
- Traffic Computer Vision
- Multimodal Large Language Models
- Traffic Congestion Detection and Prediction
- Vision-Language Models for Transportation
- Traffic State Understanding and Reasoning

### Current Research

My current research explores how specialized traffic perception models and multimodal large language models can be integrated to achieve more comprehensive traffic intelligence, from low-level visual perception to high-level traffic reasoning and prediction.

I am particularly interested in building **reliable, interpretable, and generalizable multimodal AI systems for intelligent transportation**.
