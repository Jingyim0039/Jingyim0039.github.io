I am a Ph.D. researcher at the **School of Civil Engineering and Transportation, South China University of Technology (SCUT)**, with research interests spanning **intelligent transportation, traffic vision, multimodal artificial intelligence, and expressway traffic analysis**.

My current work focuses on visual traffic perception under challenging conditions, expressway congestion recognition and prediction, and the integration of specialized traffic models with multimodal large language models for higher-level traffic understanding.

### Research Interests

- Intelligent Transportation Systems
- Traffic Computer Vision
- Multimodal Large Language Models
- Expressway Traffic Perception
- Congestion Recognition and Prediction
- Vision-Language Models for Transportation
