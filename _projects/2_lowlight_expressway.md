This research focuses on reliable expressway traffic perception under **low illumination, headlight glare, and complex nighttime traffic conditions**.

The work investigates adaptive image recognition and attention-enhanced visual models for vehicle perception and congestion recognition, with an emphasis on real-world expressway deployment.
