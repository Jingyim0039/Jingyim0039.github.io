**TrafficSeer** integrates traffic visual perception, multiscale temporal modeling, and multimodal large language models for expressway traffic analysis.

The framework targets current traffic-state recognition, future congestion prediction, and congestion-cause understanding, aiming to connect low-level perception with higher-level traffic reasoning.
