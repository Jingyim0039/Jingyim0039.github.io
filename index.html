About

Building robust perception for safer, smarter mobility.

I am a Ph.D. student in Civil and Hydraulic Engineering, with a focus on transportation, at South China University of Technology. I am also supported as a joint Ph.D. student through the Overseas Study Program of the Guangzhou Elite Project.

My current work brings together intelligent transportation systems, traffic vision, low-light image processing, and large language models. I am particularly interested in practical methods that remain dependable under difficult real-world conditions.

Research focus

From challenging visual scenes to actionable traffic insight.

My research sits at the intersection of transportation engineering and artificial intelligence.

01

Low-light traffic vision

Adaptive recognition methods for congestion and road-scene understanding when illumination is limited.

  • Feature refinement
  • Attention mechanisms
  • Congestion recognition
02

Intelligent transportation

Data-driven tools for expressway operations, complex traffic environments, and resilient mobility systems.

03

Multimodal intelligence

Exploring image processing, generative models, graph learning, and LLM-enabled transportation applications.
